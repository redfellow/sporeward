# Sporeward — Game concept

Sporeward turns human-powered outdoor activity into a shared effort to clean a changing outbreak world. Its first product slice is an Android native-recording experience connected to an account-owned test world. Code and replay evidence show substantial implemented behavior; background recording reliability, broad field acceptance, group play and production readiness remain separate gates in the [development roadmap](development-roadmap.md).

## Player experience

The first priority is to make an ordinary journey from A to B more worthwhile through optional detours, sometimes to unfamiliar places. The next priority is motivating an outing that otherwise would not happen. Score growth and competition support those experiences. Exploration stays renewable: familiar routes, commutes and short local outings should remain worthwhile without requiring progressively longer trips. These are product priorities, not numeric acceptance thresholds; see [product direction](product-direction.md) for proposed evaluations.

The intended loop is to inspect nearby opportunities, record a human-powered outing, visit reachable outbreaks or Field Labs, receive validated points and cleaning in the shared world, then choose another outing as the world changes. Field Labs provide personal resistance recovery within the confirmed gameplay relationship. A fixed reset, guaranteed recovery amount or resistance-free week is not promised.

## Product contract

OpenSpec owns normative behavior. Use the [capability specifications](../openspec/specs/) for renewable exploration, activity/contact rules, scoring, outbreak lifecycle and visuals, Field Labs, Outbreak Resistance, and the account and outing-feedback contracts introduced by the active product-requirements change. This page records the idea and rationale; it does not restate detailed thresholds or acceptance scenarios.

For MVP scoring, an activity originates in Sporeward's native recorder and is submitted for authoritative validation. The browser currently presents rendering studies rather than the full playable product. Strava import and expanded rankings are post-MVP; their eligibility, reconciliation and settlement rules remain open. Live presence, visibility controls, protection areas, route-planner integration, and strategic splitting also need separate product decisions before they can be treated as available behavior.

## Design principles

- Prefer a reachable, worthwhile detour over distance for its own sake.
- Keep nearby exploration useful and renewable across the week.
- Make the result understandable: distinguish confirmed points from conditional rewards and show meaningful changes to the world.
- Preserve account attribution and shared-world contributions without implying that activity routes or live locations are publicly visible.
- Treat implementation, automated or replay verification, emulator/phone review, outdoor reliability, and production readiness as different evidence stages.

## Open design work

Detailed gameplay questions belong with their owning capability or a future scoped proposal. Current open topics include final lifecycle and splitting behavior, late/offline event settlement, privacy boundaries, and the form of future integrations and rankings. Candidate values in experiments and archived changes remain provisional unless a capability records their acceptance. See [future ideas](future-ideas.md) for the explicitly deferred post-MVP goals and [finished goals](finished-goals.md) for dated evidence and its limits.
