# Field Labs integration

Field Labs are opt-in through `WorldStore.prepareFieldLabs()` and require the server's existing public-access cells. They persist separately from outbreak geometry, density, cooldowns and cleanup. Their provisional versioned configuration is [`FIELD_LAB_RULES`](../apps/server/src/field-labs.ts): two shared destinations, seven-day lifetime and two-kilometre minimum separation, compared with the reference world's twenty ordinary outbreaks and their shorter normal lifecycle. Seed, lifecycle epoch and accessible cell determine identity. New epochs create new identities; the current snapshot retains expired destinations for historical matching.

Placement uses cells already containing checked public way geometry. The full unrounded cell is the contact footprint, so visiting its traversable path can reach it; its central map marker is illustrative and does not assert that the exact centre is traversable or that a real station exists there. The access dataset still limits coverage and freshness. No routing or private-land access is granted by this feature.

`/world` returns shared unexpired destinations with stable identity, name, position, footprint, spawn and expiry times, plus owner-specific `collected`. Different accounts see the same public geometry. Field startup leaves labs disabled. The isolated feature bench enables them through `REPLAY_FIELD_LABS=1`; activation requires `prepareFieldLabs()` and resistance scoring, with no field deployment implied.

## Offers and collections

`POST /outings/:id/lab-offer` accepts an empty object and binds an owner-only, server-issued offer to the recording/activity ID. Existing outing IDs always return their original offer; movement and repeated requests cannot replace its reference. The quote includes world start, rules version, expiry, established centre and per-lab distance/fraction. Client-supplied centres and multipliers are rejected. Offer lifetime is 24 hours; lab validity is additionally checked at recorded contact time. Use a new activity ID for a new outing. Grouping multiple recordings into one outing remains outside the existing recorder contract.

Normal activity upload/completion validates the recorded track and existing 500-metre minimum. Lab matching reuses timestamped footprints, the ten-metre contact tolerance and the two-second same-section interpolation boundary. Accuracy values follow the existing recording/protocol validation; this adds no new GPS-quality guarantee. Outbreak contacts and lab contacts settle in one recorded-time order and one world transaction. A lab does not consume ordinary cooldown or cleanup. An expired-at-contact lab is rejected; a contact recorded before expiry can settle afterward within the activity import window. Lab settlement rechecks that the activity ended within 24 hours of current processing, even when a previously stored upload receipt was eligible.

Offline visits remain provisional before upload. A cached quote is usable when it belongs to this owner/activity/world and was valid at contact. Without such a quote, upload performs authoritative reconciliation using a server-derived reference and labels the confirmed result `offerSource: reconciled`; there is no guaranteed pre-upload amount. A valid original quote uses `quoted`. The recap's `labCollections` records the persisted flavour name, advertised maximum, actual fraction and actual present resistance removed. All ten names are mechanically identical. `limited` signals a smaller fraction or no available resistance; interfaces must explain diminishing successive collections or exhausted resistance rather than promise a stored item.

Collection identity is owner + lab within the world snapshot. A retry of the same activity returns its stored result. A different activity revisiting that lab reports `already-collected` with zero new restoration. The shared lab stays visible, personally disabled, until expiry; friends retain independent opportunities. Distinct concurrent outing offers share the same authoritative collection load. Diminishing recovery is timed from settlement, so backdating contacts cannot erase it. Restoration records the contribution identities actually available at settlement; a later historical upload cannot silently enlarge an earlier collection. Confirmed ordinary awards and collection recap amounts remain fixed. Present reductions account for passive decay between contact and settlement.
