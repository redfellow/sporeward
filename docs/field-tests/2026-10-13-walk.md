# Walking recorder comparison — 13 October 2026

Compared the uploaded Sporeward recording with a Garmin Forerunner 955 FIT recording exported from Strava. All times below are local Tampere time (Europe/Helsinki). Raw location data is excluded from this report.

## Results

- Phone recording: 16:10:39–16:37:23, 26 minutes 44 seconds elapsed; 23 minutes 44 seconds excluding the explicit pause. It contains 1,230 fixes across two recording sections.
- Phone pause: 16:16:12–16:19:11. No fixes were recorded inside the pause. The watch accumulated about 91 metres during it; the phone endpoints were about 68 metres apart. The phone correctly excludes the connecting line from its distance calculation.
- Across 1,001 timestamp-matched coordinate pairs within one second, median phone/watch separation is 3.5 metres; the 95th percentile is 10.0 metres. In the main earlier comparison window, the 95th percentile is 6.5 metres. Neither device is a ground-truth reference.
- Phone sampling is normally approximately once per second. There are 46 same-section gaps longer than two seconds, including 44 concentrated around 16:28–16:33. The player’s screenshot locates the store at the northeast turn of the route; timestamped phone positions place the store-area segment around 16:28–16:33. This matches the sparse sampling interval. The watch was paused from 16:27:18 to 16:33:11, so there is no watch reference for most of that interval. Exact indoor entry/exit times remain unknown.
- Near 16:36, matched positions diverge by up to 49 metres. The watch then loses valid coordinates at 16:36:16. Comparing the screenshot with the timestamped phone route places this disagreement after the store, near the return toward the starting area. It does not establish which device was more accurate.
- The phone reports median accuracy of 4.1 metres, with only one fix exceeding 25 metres. Reported accuracy therefore cannot by itself resolve the late disagreement between devices.
- Raw phone distance is approximately 1,631 metres. The server counts approximately 1,593 metres under the existing rule excluding connections across gaps longer than two seconds. This removes about 38 metres; it is not a corrected estimate of true distance.
- The Garmin file covers a longer outing with different pauses and reports 1,782 metres. Comparing these whole-recording totals as a measurement error would be misleading.
- A pair of phone fixes only 24 milliseconds apart produces an apparent speed of about 84 km/h from roughly half a metre of coordinate jitter. Instantaneous speed checks need to handle very short intervals rather than interpreting this as real movement.
- The successful upload is stored as one activity with two chunks. The reported interrupted attempt and retry did not create a duplicate activity. The cause of Docker crashing was not established by the recording comparison.

## Limits and follow-ups

The player reports that the screen was off for approximately 90% of the recording. Mostly regular sampling and close agreement with Garmin outside the degraded segments provide positive field evidence that screen-off recording worked as expected on this phone during this outing. Exact screen-off intervals were not logged, so this test cannot attribute each gap to screen state or assess callback delivery latency. The store-related sparse sampling should not by itself be treated as a background-recording failure. The store was described as around the 20-minute mark; elapsed, paused, and moving-time displays can refer to different moments, so this is approximate context only.

Priorities: add the requested persistent recording notification; instrument lifecycle and delivery timing for the next field test; investigate sparse sampling without bridging gaps for game contacts; test position quality and very short fix intervals before introducing speed-based validation. Retain source-specific tracks and pause boundaries when implementing the planned combined outing view.
