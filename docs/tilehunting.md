# Tile hunting

Tile hunting is an outdoor exploration game, especially popular with cyclists: visit map tiles and collect them through recorded routes.

The map uses a zoom-level-14 grid of 16,384 × 16,384 tiles (about 268 million). Physical tile size varies by latitude: roughly 2.5 km per side at the equator and 1.2 km around Tampere, Finland.

Common goals are:

- **Total tiles:** visit as many distinct tiles as possible.
- **Largest square:** complete the largest square of visited tiles, with no gaps.
- **Largest cluster:** grow a connected group of visited tiles that each have visited neighbours on all four sides; diagonal neighbours do not qualify.
- **Single-ride square:** complete the largest square during one ride.

Players plan routes through missing tiles to expand their coverage, squares, and clusters. This encourages exploration of unfamiliar roads, gravel tracks, and trails. Water or access restrictions can make tiles difficult to reach; respect private property. Cycling is common, but the author also accepts walking, rowing, and other human-powered travel.

The article describes using Strava activity records with StatsHunters or VeloViewer to map visited tiles and track progress.

Source: [Mikko Saari, “Tile hunting – perusteet” (7 June 2023)](https://www.mikkosaari.fi/tile-hunting-perusteet/).
