# Proposal

## Why

Reward worthwhile cycling detours and weekend adventures without permanently exhausting nearby places. Personal, recovering Outbreak Resistance and shared Field Labs should preserve useful short local outings while making a change of scenery valuable.

## What Changes

- Build personal geographic resistance from committed cleaning strength at one representative point per outbreak, retaining area familiarity across replacements.
- Recover resistance passively and reconcile existing repeat-point reductions into one bounded visit-reward calculation.
- Add shared, uncommon, longer-lived Field Labs, independently collectible once by each player until expiry.
- Reduce resistance globally on collection, scaling restoration by distance from an established centre with a cap, stable outing offers and diminishing successive benefits.
- First calibrate deterministic weekly scenarios; then integrate authoritative persistence, contracts and gameplay presentation using the separately approved visual work.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `outbreak-resistance`: Personal geographic resistance, passive recovery and bounded combined visit rewards.
- `field-labs`: Shared lab lifecycle, independent collection and personal restoration offers.

### Modified Capabilities

None; accepted OpenSpec baseline is currently empty. These deltas do not claim to baseline every existing scoring or recording behaviour.

## Impact

Expected work spans `packages/game`, `packages/protocol`, server world/account persistence and replay fixtures, mobile world presentation, and [concept](../../../../docs/game-concept.md)/[player rules](../../../../docs/player-rules.md). Preserve historical awards, recording ownership, contact geometry and cleanup rules. No public deployment or historical score recalculation is authorized by this proposal.

The [desktop visual study](../2026-10-21-explore-field-labs-and-outbreak-resistance/proposal.md) is proceeding independently; do not edit or reimplement its files during this planning capture. Coordinate eventual adoption with the [archived Android visual-port change](../2026-10-22-replicate-approved-visuals-and-showcase/proposal.md). Achievements, size/category rules and strategic splitting remain separate.
