# Proposal

## Why

The agreed MVP connects ordinary detours with occasional Field Lab expeditions that reduce resistance for subsequent familiar play. Existing direction and capability documents do not fully express that connection, and one balancing requirement risks turning an illustrative recovery percentage into a product target.

## What Changes

- Capture the confirmed player experience: optional A-to-B detours, renewable familiar routes and short local walks, and lab hunting that reduces later resistance-based point loss.
- Describe resistance as spatial; its summary centre follows changing cleaning geography gradually. Adventure cleaning can add contributions in the direction ridden.
- Clarify that multiple labs can add recovery subject to existing diminishing returns, without a full reset, a guaranteed percentage or a resistance-free following week.
- Update only `docs/product-direction.md` among shared documents, retaining its implementation and field-acceptance limitations.
- Keep route-choice, outing-return and comprehension observations tied to this loop, with unagreed thresholds explicitly open.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `renewable-exploration`: the MVP experience connecting optional route choices, useful familiar local activity and understandable expedition benefits.

### Modified Capabilities

- `field-labs`: replace the fixed illustrative weekend recovery target with comparative evidence of subsequent local-play benefit.
- `outbreak-resistance`: clarify local spatial reward evaluation and the gradually evolving summary centre.

## Impact

Documentation and capability contracts only: this change's artifacts, the three named capability baselines at sync/archive, and `docs/product-direction.md`. Existing code already contains spatial contribution weighting, gradual centre movement and fractional lab restoration; this change does not retune or enable it.

No runtime changes, tests, commits, push, PR, deployment or external publishing. No changes to the earlier `consolidate-sporeward-product-requirements` draft or other shared docs. Future integrations, achievements and new routing features are outside this lane. Completing this documentation change does not establish gameplay effectiveness or field reliability.
