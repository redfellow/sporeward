# Proposal

## Why

Project-wide priorities and unresolved questions should survive closing the development chats. Introductory documents also lag recorded implementation milestones, making it difficult to distinguish current capability, agreed design and remaining acceptance.

## What Changes

- Establish the agreed product priorities and renewable-exploration principle in durable project documentation.
- Reconcile README, architecture and roadmap status against source and completion evidence, preserving unresolved real-device and product acceptance.
- Define a practical route-choice evaluation alongside technical and visual acceptance.
- Maintain an explicit inventory of unanswered questions and deferred ideas, with links to their owning changes or documents instead of duplicate task lists.
- Provide a resumption map for independently progressing visual, gameplay and Android work.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None. This is documentation and handoff work; `skip_specs: true` deliberately avoids inventing a product capability for a documentation change.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Feature requirements remain in their owning changes; no gameplay or runtime behaviour is changed here.

## Impact

Future implementation updates [README](../../../README.md), [architecture](../../../docs/architecture.md), [roadmap](../../../docs/development-roadmap.md), [concept](../../../docs/game-concept.md) and a concise project-level direction/question index. The [current goal](../../../docs/current-goal.md) remains an index, not a duplicate checklist. No source-code changes, deployment or changes to other agents' active artifacts are included in this capture.

The durable decision inventory and outstanding questions are already captured in [design.md](design.md), so completing this documentation work does not depend on retrieving this chat.
