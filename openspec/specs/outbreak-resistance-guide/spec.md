# outbreak-resistance-guide Specification

## Purpose

Make renewable personal Outbreak Resistance and its recovery understandable through an isolated desktop visual demonstration.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Personal resistance geography
The guide SHALL label the effect Outbreak Resistance and display a personal circular zone strongest at its centre and fading toward its edges, using a soft blue haze at the selected 227° hue with restrained lavender contours that strengthen with resistance. Roads, ordinary outbreak states and personal contact cues SHALL remain readable beneath it.

#### Scenario: Inspect local resistance
- **WHEN** the resistance overlay is enabled
- **THEN** the tester can identify its centre, gradual falloff and affected opportunities without losing the underlying map context.

### Requirement: Renewable local opportunities
The guide SHALL demonstrate repeated local cleaning increasing resistance and reducing local point opportunities, and elapsed time passively reducing resistance. It SHALL explain that this is temporary rather than permanent geographic exhaustion.

#### Scenario: Commute and rest
- **WHEN** the tester advances illustrative repeated local cleaning and then passive recovery
- **THEN** the zone first strengthens and local rewards decline, then the zone weakens and rewards recover.

### Requirement: Global restoration feedback
The guide SHALL demonstrate a Field Lab reward reducing accumulated personal resistance everywhere, visibly weakening the zone. Restoration SHALL NOT be presented as an expiring boost or a stored inventory item.

#### Scenario: Collect a lab reward
- **WHEN** the tester collects an available lab with positive restoration
- **THEN** the resistance display visibly weakens and local point opportunities improve without selecting a recovery neighbourhood.

### Requirement: Honest preview scope
The guide SHALL label balancing values and gameplay interactions as illustrative, provide repeatable reset, and avoid changing accounts, scores, recordings or field-world state. Ordinary outbreak examples SHALL retain their accepted visual treatment.

#### Scenario: Repeat the demonstration
- **WHEN** the tester resets after simulated commuting and lab collection
- **THEN** the original example state returns and no live gameplay state has changed.
