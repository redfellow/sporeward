# renewable-exploration Specification

## Purpose

Connect optional A-to-B detours and occasional Field Lab expeditions with renewable, worthwhile familiar local play in the agreed MVP.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Player-chosen optional detours
The MVP SHALL present reachable world opportunities and their available rewards and eligibility so a player can choose an optional detour during an A-to-B journey. Opportunities SHALL support geographic variety without requiring a permanent exploration unlock. This contract SHALL NOT require automatic routing, a fixed detour distance or a reward on every direct route.

#### Scenario: Compare plausible routes
- **WHEN** a player inspects an ordinary A-to-B journey with reachable targets along alternative routes
- **THEN** the player can inspect those opportunities and choose whether their rewards justify a detour, including one toward an unfamiliar place.

### Requirement: Useful recurring local play
Otherwise eligible familiar-route visits and short local outings SHALL retain bounded ordinary visit rewards as personal resistance accumulates. Passive recovery and renewable world opportunities SHALL allow continued local play without permanent tile exhaustion. Longer expeditions SHALL provide optional additional benefit rather than become mandatory maintenance for earning local rewards.

#### Scenario: Continue the weekday routine
- **WHEN** a player repeats familiar commutes or rides without a weekend lab expedition
- **THEN** eligible visits remain useful under the existing bounded reward rules and are not permanently exhausted.

#### Scenario: Take a local walk
- **WHEN** a player takes a thirty-minute local walk that meets the existing activity and contact eligibility rules
- **THEN** eligible nearby opportunities can contribute to useful play without requiring a distant geographic unlock or lab collection first.

### Requirement: Understandable expedition benefit
Field Lab feedback SHALL explain confirmed resistance reduction and possible later benefit to familiar-area ordinary visit points. It SHALL distinguish points from committed cleaning and conditional cleanup bonuses. It SHALL NOT predict exact points; subsequent contributions, elapsed time and reward rounding affect later points. Zero reduction SHALL make no later-benefit claim. It SHALL NOT promise a complete reset, guaranteed percentage, stored future credit or a resistance-free following week.

#### Scenario: Return after lab hunting
- **WHEN** a confirmed Field Lab collection reduces resistance accumulated around the player's usual cleaning area
- **THEN** the recap explains that the reduction may lessen resistance-based point loss on later eligible familiar-area visits compared with equivalent play without it, without predicting exact points and subject to later contributions, elapsed time and reward rounding.

#### Scenario: Confirmed collection restores no resistance
- **WHEN** a confirmed Field Lab collection has zero resistance reduction
- **THEN** the recap says no resistance was restored in that collection and makes no claim that it benefits later visit points.

#### Scenario: Collect several labs
- **WHEN** a player collects multiple eligible labs during an expedition
- **THEN** feedback explains their actual cumulative recovery and existing diminishing returns without promising a fixed recovery level.
